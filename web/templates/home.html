What makes this different

You cannot skip ahead

A lesson opens only when the previous one is passed. The only shortcut is a {{ skip_pass }}% skip exam that proves you already know the module.

Practice gates the quiz

Reading is not learning. The quiz stays locked until the lesson's practice task actually passes its checks in the sandbox.

Three explanation languages

English, हिन्दी and Hinglish, switchable per account. Code stays in English, the explanation adapts to you.

Runs on your machine

No CDN, no telemetry, no third-party fonts. One SQLite file holds the accounts and the progress.
